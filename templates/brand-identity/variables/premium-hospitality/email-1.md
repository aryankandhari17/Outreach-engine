## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about direct bookings this year?

In premium hospitality, the property is often better than the brand suggests. Guests can't quite feel the difference until they arrive, and by then the booking decision was already made on price and reviews instead of pull.

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand pull this year?

In hospitality marketing, the property has to start selling before the guest ever arrives. When the digital identity feels lighter than the actual stay, the booking decision drifts to price, reviews, and OTAs.

---

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

In hospitality, the digital and pre-arrival expression usually has more to give. The on-property design carries the experience, but the brand world before the guest arrives often feels lighter than the stay itself.
