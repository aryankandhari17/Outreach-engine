## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about enrolment this year?

For premium institutions, the website and prospectus often publish a lot. But families still leave with the same hesitations they came with, and end up reducing the school to rankings, fees, and brochure claims.

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand pull this year?

In education marketing, the institution publishes a lot but still leaves families doing most of the trust-building themselves. Conversion to enrolment then depends on counsellors and rankings instead of the brand pull that should be doing the work.

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

For premium institutions, the brand often defaults to the same codes as everyone else: global, holistic, future-ready. The harder creative work is showing the actual character of the place in a way that doesn't sound like the prospectus next to it.
