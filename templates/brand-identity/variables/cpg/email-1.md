## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about shelf recall this year?

In CPG, the hard part isn't being seen. It's being remembered after someone walks past five similar products on a shelf or scrolls past a dozen on Instagram. Most brands look polished and forgettable at the same time.

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand memory this year?

In CPG marketing, the goal isn't really visibility, it's recall. Most brands invest in being seen and underinvest in being remembered, and the difference shows up in repeat rates and shelf preference.

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

In CPG, the brief is mostly recall, but most packaging design optimises for polish instead. The work that actually gets remembered tends to break a few category conventions on purpose.
