## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about ticket momentum this year?

For most events and experience brands, the brand has to do the emotional work before the event itself can. When the identity feels generic, the anticipation that drives ticket sales never quite builds.

---

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand anticipation this year?

In events marketing, the brand has to manufacture anticipation before there's anything to experience. When the identity feels generic, ticket sales lean on price and FOMO instead of pull.

---

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

For experience brands, the identity has to make the audience feel something before they've experienced anything. Most event branding decorates the show; the strong ones build the anticipation that fills the seats.
