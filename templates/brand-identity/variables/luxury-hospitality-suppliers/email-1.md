## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about your brand this year?

For most luxury hospitality suppliers, the brand has to do something quietly difficult — feel worthy of sitting next to Hermès, Bulgari, or Le Labo in a Four Seasons bathroom. When the identity doesn't quite carry that weight, hotels either pass entirely or use the brand only for lower-tier properties.

---

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand strategy this year?

In the luxury hospitality supply space, the brand is the entire sales argument. Hotels aren't comparing on price or formulation; they're deciding whether the brand belongs in their guest experience. Marketing investment then has to keep proving what a stronger identity could be doing on its own.

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

For luxury hospitality suppliers, the brand has to look like it earned its place in the room. Hotels make the call in seconds, against globally recognised names. When the identity feels even slightly aspirational rather than authored, the supplier ends up in the wrong tier of properties.
