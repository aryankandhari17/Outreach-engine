## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about repeat visits this year?

In F&B, the brand stops doing work the moment the meal leaves the room. On a delivery app, in a takeaway bag, on a thumbnail, the restaurant is reduced to a price and a logo. The full experience that earns loyalty in-store rarely survives the channel.

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand recall this year?

In F&B marketing, the brand often peaks in-store and quietly disappears the moment delivery, takeaway, or aggregator ordering takes over. The marketing investment builds loyalty in one channel and gets flattened in another.

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

For F&B brands, the parts of the identity that matter most are the ones that travel: the bag, the box, the menu, the thumbnail. In-store does most of the emotional work, but those touchpoints have to carry the brand on their own.
