## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about your brand this year?

For most interior and architecture studios, the work is stronger than how the brand currently shows up. The portfolio, the website, the identity. They often feel a step behind the actual quality of the projects.

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand strategy this year?

In design and architecture marketing, the work is usually stronger than the brand presence around it. New business depends on word of mouth and portfolio, while the brand itself underrepresents the studio.

---

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

For most design studios, the brand around the work usually feels a step behind the work itself. The projects deserve a stronger identity system, but the studio rarely gets the time to build one.
