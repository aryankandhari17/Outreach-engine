## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about consult conversion this year?

In wellness and aesthetics, people aren't just asking "does this work?" They're asking "can I trust this with my face, my body, my health?" The brand has to answer that before any conversation starts, and most don't quite do enough of that work.

---

## cmo

**Subject:** Thinking about brand trust this year?

In wellness marketing, the gap between leads and consults is usually a brand problem, not a funnel one. People hesitate because the brand hasn't quite signaled enough trust before the booking moment.

---

## creative-director

**Subject:** Thinking about brand impact this year?

For wellness brands, the creative challenge is unusual. The identity has to carry warmth and credibility at the same time, in a space where most competitors lean too clinical or too pretty. Holding both is where the brand becomes trustworthy.
