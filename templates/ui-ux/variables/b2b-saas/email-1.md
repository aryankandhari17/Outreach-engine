## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about activation this year?

A lot of SaaS teams are not losing users because the product lacks value. They lose them because new users don't quickly reach the moment where the product clearly helps them do something faster, save time, make a better decision, or improve a business outcome.

So signups turn into non-usage, and the product doesn't get a fair chance to prove itself.
