**Subject:** Thinking about direct bookings this year?

For most hotel groups, the painful question isn't traffic. It's why guests still trust Booking or Expedia more than the hotel's own site to book the same room. The direct experience tends to feel weaker, even when the rate is better.

For most hotel groups, the direct booking product has slowly fallen behind what guests are used to on Booking or Expedia. Not in features, but in how easy the decision feels.

## design-head

**Subject:** Thinking about design's impact this year?

For hotel groups, the OTA flow has set a quiet standard most direct sites haven't caught up to. Not in features, but in how easy deciding feels. Design carries most of that gap, even when teams call it a product problem.
