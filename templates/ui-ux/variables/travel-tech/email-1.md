## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about booking conversion this year?

For travel and experience platforms, the sale isn't usually lost when people are dreaming. It's lost the moment they need certainty. Availability, cancellation, confirmation. When any of that feels shaky, they book elsewhere or not at all.

## cpo

**Subject:** Thinking about the booking product this year?

In travel tech, the part of the product that needs to be strongest is often the part that gets the least design attention. The booking moment, where availability, cancellation, and confirmation all need to feel certain. That's where most platforms quietly lose people.

## design-head

**Subject:** Thinking about design's impact this year?

In travel platforms, design tends to peak where users are inspired and quiet down where users are deciding. Cancellation, availability, confirmation. The certainty layer is often where design has the most to give and gets the least time to give it.
