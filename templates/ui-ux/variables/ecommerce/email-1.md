## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about conversion this year?

In ecommerce, people don't usually leave because checkout is broken. They leave because they're still unsure about the product, the value, or whether buying now feels worth it.

## cpo

**Subject:** Thinking about the product side this year?

In ecommerce, the part that quietly hurts conversion isn't usually the checkout itself. It's that the product experience leading up to it doesn't do enough to make buying feel worth it.

---

## design-head

**Subject:** Thinking about design's impact this year?

In ecommerce, the conversion lift that design can drive often sits in the parts of the experience that aren't the loudest priorities. The product page details, the trust cues, the moment between consideration and click. These quietly do most of the work.
