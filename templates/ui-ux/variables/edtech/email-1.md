## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about retention this year?

In edtech, sign-ups aren't usually the hard part. The hard part is that parents pay, students lose interest after a week, and renewal turns into a much harder conversation than the first sale was.

## cpo

**Subject:** Thinking about retention this year?

In edtech, the product carries an unusual load. Parents are watching it, students are using it, and they want different things from the same screens. When the product can only really serve one of them well, retention quietly suffers.

## design-head

**Subject:** Thinking about design's impact this year?

In edtech, design has to serve two very different users on the same screens. Parents who want visibility, students who want momentum. Most edtech design picks one and quietly underserves the other.
