## ceo

**Subject:** Thinking about patient conversion this year?

In healthtech, people don't usually leave because the product is bad. They leave because they're not sure if it's safe, right for them, or worth trusting with something this personal.

---

## cpo

**Subject:** Thinking about activation this year?

In healthtech, activation often stalls because the product gets the medical part right but doesn't quite carry the user through the emotional one. People hesitate before booking, and the product rarely meets them there.

---

## design-head

**Subject:** Thinking about design's impact this year?

In healthtech, design has to do something most categories don't ask of it. Carry warmth and credibility at the same time, in flows where users are anxious. Most healthtech screens are correct. Fewer feel reassuring.
